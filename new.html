Who I am

An engineer at the intersection of electronics, AI, software and cybersecurity.

I'm an Electronics and Computer Science graduate currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity at St. Joseph's University. My work sits at the intersection of electronics, intelligent software and security — from embedded systems and industrial automation to machine learning and cybersecurity.

I've worked on industrial control panels, built embedded and IoT systems, developed machine learning solutions, and explored web application security. This combination of hardware, software and security gives me a practical approach to understanding and building technology end to end.

Beyond engineering, I work with video editing and robotics activities, experiences that have taught me to turn ideas into outcomes while working with real deadlines, constraints and teams.
