Who I am

An engineer who moves between hardware, software and security.

I'm an Electronics and Computer Science student, currently reading for an M.Sc. in Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence at St Joseph's University. My work sits at the intersection of automation, embedded systems and intelligent software — I've commissioned industrial control panels, trained machine learning models, and probed web applications for vulnerabilities, often in the same month.

Outside engineering, I edit video and lead logistics for a robotics society — both of which taught me to ship under real deadlines with real constraints.
