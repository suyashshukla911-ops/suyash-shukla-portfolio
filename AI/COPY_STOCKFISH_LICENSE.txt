ChessMind optionally uses Stockfish 19 as the native chess calculation backend.

Stockfish is free and open source software released under the GNU General Public
License, version 3 (GPLv3). This project downloads the official binary at setup
rather than redistributing a modified Stockfish executable.

Official source/download information:
https://stockfishchess.org/download/
https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish/releases/tag/sf_19
